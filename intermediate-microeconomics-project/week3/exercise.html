What these exercises test

These exercises test whether you can formulate and solve the consumer’s problem. You will use the budget constraint, Cobb–Douglas expenditure shares and the tangency condition to calculate an optimal bundle, then verify that it is feasible, exhausts the budget and lies on the highest attainable indifference curve.

The complex-pricing exercise tests what changes when the budget line has a kink. Instead of applying one tangency condition mechanically, derive the constraint in each pricing region, calculate the valid interior candidates, include the kink and feasible corners, and compare their utility levels.
