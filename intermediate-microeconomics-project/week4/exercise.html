Exercise for week 4

What these exercises test

These exercises test comparative statics in consumer choice. You will calculate how a sales tax, an income tax or a change in the interest rate alters the budget constraint and the optimal bundle. You must distinguish a change in relative prices from a change in purchasing power and explain the result using economic reasoning.

The exercises also test revenue-equivalent tax comparisons and the decomposition of a total response into substitution and income effects. Before interpreting any compensated bundle, identify whether the exercise uses Hicksian or Slutsky compensation and make sure the graph’s transfer is set to the required benchmark.
