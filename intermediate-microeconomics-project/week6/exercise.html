Week 6 – Exercise

Exercise for week 1

What this exercise tests

This exercise tests decision-making under risk when the possible outcomes and their probabilities are known. You will compare expected monetary payoff with expected utility, examine the trade-off between a larger potential reward and a greater probability of loss, and connect the chosen level of risk to the curvature of the utility function.

The task also tests interpretation. A choice that does not maximise expected money is not necessarily irrational: a risk-averse person may prefer a safer option because it delivers greater expected utility. At the same time, one observed choice should be treated as evidence about risk preferences rather than a complete measurement of them.
