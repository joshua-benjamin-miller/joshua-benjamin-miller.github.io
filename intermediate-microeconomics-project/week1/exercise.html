Exercise for week 1

What these exercises test

These exercises test the mathematical and marginal reasoning used throughout the subject. You will construct net benefit from total benefit and total cost, use derivatives to obtain marginal benefit and marginal cost, and determine whether a candidate is a maximum, minimum or boundary solution.

You should also be able to evaluate functions and partial derivatives, solve a nonlinear system and reject algebraic solutions that are infeasible in the economic domain. The main objective is to connect the calculation with the graph: the net-benefit maximum occurs where its slope changes sign, not where total benefit equals total cost.
